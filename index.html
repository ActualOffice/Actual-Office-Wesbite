Actual Office is a London and Belfast based architecture and interior design studio delivering considered designs for thoughtful clients across residential and luxury fit-out projects. Conceptual clarity, craftsmanship and human experience guide everything we do.

Our work balances rigorous thinking with an exploratory design process, informed by technical expertise and an intuitive understanding of construction and detail.

Working closely with specialist makers, we celebrate materials for their inherent qualities and ability to endure, creating spaces that enhance the everyday.

Our work has received recognition across the architecture and design community, both in the UK and internationally.
